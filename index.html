Arriving from the Airport to your accommodation.

There is a dedicated bus service from the Airport to the City Centre. For more Information, you can download the Buses and Trams App or use the transportation link below to help you plan your travel. They will show you the busses’ numbers that serve your area, their availability, routes, map etc Or you can use the following Links:

Edinburgh Airport Transport

From the City Center to 103 Restalrig Road

Then from the City Centre to your accommodation (103 Restalrig Road) you can take any of the buses No 19, 34, 25, 12 During the day and 25, 25 N (night busses!)

  • Daytime buses: 25, 34, or 49
  • Night buses: 25 or N25

Arriving from the City Centre Train Station (Waverley Train Station) to your accommodation @ 103 Restalrig Road

Again, please use the App or the link https://lothianbuses.co.uk/ to plan your journey.
Then you can catch bus number 19, 34, or 12. There will be a bit of walk, please see the link for buses.
For night buses, you can use 25 or 25N, which run direct from the apartment to City Centre and vice versa.

  • Daytime buses: 19, 34, 12
  • Night buses: 25, 25N (direct)
